Privacy

What this service keeps about you, and why.

What is stored

  • Your account: the name you chose, and your email address if you sign in with Google, Apple or GitHub. Plus when the account was created and when you last logged in.
  • Your passkeys: their public keys only. The private key never leaves your device.
  • Logins: sessions, connected apps (such as claude.ai) and personal tokens, stored only as one-way hashes, with when they were created and last used.
  • Your models and files: the recipes, their history, your comments and the STL files. They are private to you; administrators of the service can see them to keep it running.

What is not stored

Your IP address is used only for a moment, to limit how many accounts can be created from one network and to work out which country you log in from. It is not saved. For each login we keep the country, the time, how long you stay logged in and your browser's name, which administrators can see; these records are deleted after 90 days, or with your account. There are no tracking or advertising cookies; the one cookie keeps you logged in.

Others involved

When you create an account, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are not a bot. When you sign in with Google, Apple or GitHub, that provider tells us your account id and email. When you connect Claude or ChatGPT, that app reads and changes your models on your behalf; what it does with them is covered by its own terms.

If you use Send feedback, the text you write is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to be tidied into an issue, and, once you confirm the draft, to GitHub, where the issue is created in the project's repository. Your name and email are not added to it. Do not write personal details in feedback.

Deleting your data

Open Account settings from the menu under your avatar (top right) and choose Delete account. Your account, models, files, passkeys and tokens are removed at once. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
